# Anti-Pattern: Perfectionism

Never publishing a note because it's "not ready" stops all progress. A good note today beats a perfect note never.

## The Problem

Perfectionism leads to:
- No new notes for weeks or months
- Notes constantly being revised
- No iteration — vault stagnates while chasing impossible standards
- Notes remain in "draft" forever

The alternative is progress — capture what's useful now, refine later.

## Signs of Perfectionism

- Notes remain in "draft" status for >30 days
- More than 14 days since a new note was created
- Single notes revised >5 times without progressing to "active"
- Revisions never complete

## The Solution: Progress Over Perfection

Write for your future self, not for perfection.

- Use [[Note Lifecycle Management|iterate later]]
- Capture the core idea, refine over time
- Done is better than perfect

## The Refinement Cycle

Notes evolve through stages:

1. **Whisper** — Initial capture, rough notes
2. **Draft** — Structured but unverified
3. **Refined** — Verified and polished
4. **Evergreen** — Stable, well-connected, regularly reviewed

Not every note needs to reach "evergreen." Some stay useful at "draft."

## When to Publish

Capture when:
- You understand enough to be useful
- The note would help your future self
- You can explain the core idea

Refine when:
- You learn something that updates the note
- You find the note is hard to understand
- Others will use it

## The Test

Ask: "Would my future self thank me for having this note?"

If yes, capture it. Even if rough.

## Why Perfectionism Fails

1. **Time costs** — Perfecting takes forever, delays capture
2. **Requirements shift** — What you think matters changes
3. **Iteration improves** — Refining after use is more effective than pre-perfecting

Think of notes as living documents, not finished products.

## Test for Perfectionism Anti-Pattern

To identify perfectionism in your vault:

1. **Draft Age Test**: Are any notes in "draft" status older than 30 days?
2. **Capture Rate Test**: Has it been more than 14 days since a new note was created?
3. **Revision Loop Test**: Have any single notes been revised more than 5 times without progressing to "active" status?

If you answer yes to 2+ of these questions, you may be suffering from perfectionism.
