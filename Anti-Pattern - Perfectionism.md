# Anti-Pattern: Perfectionism

> **Redirected (2026-03-29):** Content consolidated into [[Best Practice - Progress Over Perfection]].
> This anti-pattern note contained identical content to its counterpart. The vault now has a single reference with both positive and negative framing — see the best practice note for full guidance.

## Quick Summary

**What:** Never publishing a note because it's "not ready" stops all progress.

**Signs:**
- No new notes for weeks or months
- Notes constantly being revised without completion
- No iteration — vault stagnates

**Solution:** Progress over perfection — capture useful now, refine later.

## The Two-Phase Workflow

The capture-and-process separation from [[Best Practice - Selective Capture]] solves perfectionism:

1. **Capture mode** — Get the idea down, any format. Don't judge. Don't refine.
2. **Process mode** — Later, transform into atomic notes with structure.

"Ready" is not a prerequisite for capture — it's a prerequisite for publishing to others.

## The Lifecycle

Notes evolve through stages:

1. **Whisper** — Initial rough capture
2. **Draft** — Structured but unverified
3. **Refined** — Verified and polished
4. **Evergreen** — Stable, well-connected, regularly reviewed

Not every note needs to reach "Evergreen." Some stay useful at "Draft."

## When to Capture

Capture when:
- You understand enough to be useful to your future self
- The note would help answer a question you have
- You can explain the core idea in one sentence

## Test

**"Would my future self thank me for having this note, even rough?"**

If yes → capture it.
