# Seed Gap: Beverage Knowledge Domain Coverage

> The vault lacks dedicated exploration of beverage knowledge as a domain category — beer, wine, tea, and mixology are underexplored compared to food domains like cooking.

## The Problem

The Seed has stress tests and frontier exploration for many domains:
- Cooking (extensive)
- Woodworking (extensive)
- Music (multiple stress tests)
- Gardening (seed stress tests)
- Photography (multiple notes)
- Medical/Legal/Financial (heavily covered)

But beverage knowledge domains are fragmented:
- **Coffee Brewing:** Has a Seed stress test (`Seed Stress Test - Link Density Rule in Coffee Brewing.md`)
- **Wine:** No dedicated stress test or frontier exploration
- **Beer Brewing:** No dedicated stress test or frontier exploration  
- **Tea:** No dedicated stress test or frontier exploration
- **Mixology:** No dedicated stress test or frontier exploration

This is surprising because beverage knowledge shares characteristics with cooking but has unique aspects:
1. **Temporal/Aging knowledge** — wine aging, beer aging, tea aging
2. **Sensory evaluation** — tasting notes, aroma profiles, flavor wheels
3. **Equipment specificity** — brewing equipment, wine making, distillation
4. **Ingredient transformation** — fermentation as a knowledge type

## What's Missing

The vault should have:
1. Frontier exploration of beverage knowledge bases (covering wine, beer, tea, mixology)
2. Seed stress tests for at least one beverage domain beyond coffee
3. Guidance on sensory evaluation knowledge capture for beverages
4. Temporal knowledge handling for aging beverages

## Why This Matters

Beverage knowledge is a legitimate domain that people build knowledge bases around:
- Wine collectors and enthusiasts
- Home brewers (beer, mead, cider)
- Tea connoisseurs
- Mixologists and cocktail enthusiasts

The same Seed rules should work for these domains, but without stress tests, we don't know if they do.

## Proposed Action

Either:
1. Add frontier exploration for beverage knowledge bases, OR
2. Add at least one seed stress test for a beverage domain (wine or beer), OR
3. Document why beverages are intentionally excluded (if they are)
