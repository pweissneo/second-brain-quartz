# Anti-Pattern: Over-Organization

> This anti-pattern has been merged into [[Best Practice - Minimal Organization]].
> The content about over-organization (failure modes, signs, solutions) is now captured in that best practice note.

## Why This Exists

This note remains as a redirect to preserve any links pointing here. The anti-pattern content was merged into the best practice because:
- The anti-pattern and best practice covered the same ground (folder vs. wikilink structure)
- Positive framing is more actionable than negative framing
- Having both creates maintenance burden (two places to update)

## What Was Covered

The original anti-pattern covered:
- Creating folders before content exists
- Pre-planning hub structure instead of letting it emerge
- Spending more time organizing than learning
- Imposing hierarchy on knowledge that doesn't fit hierarchies

All of this is now covered in [[Best Practice - Minimal Organization]].
