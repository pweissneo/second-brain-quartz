## Original Content (Archived)

The Seed now contains the constraint interaction rule:

**Rule:** For knowledge where multiple constraints interact (time + budget + skill + equipment), create intersection notes capturing the emergent solution space, not just the union of constraint-specific knowledge.

**Why:** Single-constraint hubs miss interaction effects. A student who is both time-poor AND budget-constrained needs different advice than either constraint alone would suggest.

**Test:** For a domain with constraints A, B, C: Can you identify notes addressing A+B, B+C, A+C, AND A+B+C?
