# Seed Stress Test: 5:1 Rule in Music Composition

**Domain tested:** Music Composition  
**Rule tested:** "Use the 5:1 personal-to-general knowledge ratio"  
**Date:** 2026-03-18

## Analysis

### Does this rule make sense for music composition?

**Partially.** In music composition:
- **General knowledge** is abundant and essential: music theory, harmony rules, orchestration, form structures, notation conventions
- **Personal knowledge** includes: original techniques you've developed, compositions you've created, insights from your creative process, decisions about your own style

The challenge: A composer needs massive general knowledge before producing meaningful personal knowledge. A medical professional can have 5:1 because they generate personal insights from daily practice. A beginning composer must learn theory before creating.

### Is the rule's Test executable?

**Test:** "Pick 10 recent notes. For each: (1) Could a web search replace this note in <30 seconds? (2) Does it contain original insight, decision, or experience?"

**Problem:** In music:
- Music theory IS searchable (you can find "sonata form" in seconds)
- But mastering it requires years of study
- Personal creative decisions (why you chose that chord) are not searchable but may not qualify as "insight" under the current test

### Edge cases identified

1. **Foundational-learning domains:** Music composition, language learning, mathematics require high general-to-personal ratio initially. The 5:1 ratio applies AFTER foundational knowledge is established, not during learning.

2. **Tacit knowledge dominance:** Much musical knowledge is tacit — you cannot just read about "hearing harmony," you must develop it through practice. This knowledge is personal but not "decisions or experiences" — it's perceptual ability development.

3. **Canonical repertoire as proxy:** In music, studying existing compositions (general knowledge) is how you develop personal style. A note about "Beethoven's harmonic language" is general but creates the foundation for personal composition.

## Proposed Edge Case

**Edge case (NEW - stress test 2026-03-18):** In creative skill domains where mastery requires extensive general knowledge as prerequisite (music composition, visual art, creative writing, choreography), the 5:1 ratio needs domain-specific calibration:

- **Learning phase:** Allow higher general ratio (3:1 or 2:1) during foundational skill acquisition
- **Mastery phase:** Shift to 5:1 as personal insights emerge from practice
- **Tacit knowledge exemption:** Perceptual and kinesthetic knowledge (developing ears, feel, intuition) counts as personal even when not framed as "decisions or experiences"

**Modified test for creative domains:**
For music/art/writing notes: (1) Does this represent transferable insight YOU developed? (2) Does it document a specific creative decision you made? (3) Is it perceptual/kinesthetic knowledge from YOUR practice? (4) Would a beginner need this before producing their own work?

Count as "personal-equivalent" if yes to 3-4 (foundational learning materials that enable future personal creation).
